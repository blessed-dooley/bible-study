Privacy & reader controls

In plain language: what leaves your device, what stays on it, and how to change either.

On this pageThe short versionWhat is collectedReader controlsNotificationsMistakes

The short version

There is no account, no login, no email list, and no way for anyone here to know what you have read. Three things are worth knowing in more detail: a cookieless page counter, an optional daily notification, and two preferences kept on your device.

  • Nothing you read is tied to you. No account exists, so no reading history exists.
  • Notifications are opt-in and can be turned off at any time.
  • Your theme and red-letter settings never leave your device.

What is collected

Every data flow on this site
What Why Where it goes
Page views To know roughly how many people read, and which studies GoatCounter. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no advertising identifiers, no profile
Notification token Only if you opt in — the address your device is reachable at Firebase Cloud Messaging; the token is stored in Firestore alongside a truncated browser description
Notification choice So you are not asked twice A cookie on this site, holding only “yes” or “no”
Theme & red letter So the site looks the same next visit Your browser’s local storage. Never sent anywhere
Archive study list To list published studies Requested from this site. If that list is unavailable the page falls back to GitHub, which then sees the request
Fonts Typefaces used across the site Currently the Google Fonts service, which therefore sees the request
i Notification records do not have a fixed expiration date. Turning notifications off here attempts to remove the device token from Firestore and Firebase. The morning sender also removes registrations that Firebase reports as invalid. GoatCounter and Google Fonts handle requests under their own privacy policies.

Reader controls

Everything you can change, in one place. Each of these is also in the menu at the top of any page.

Theme

Dark for low light, Paper for long reading, Light for bright rooms. Remembered on this device.

Words of Jesus in red

When off, His words stay marked by their quotation marks. Printed pages always use black.

Morning reminder

A morning reminder for that day’s study, if you choose to receive it.

Manage
Clear what this site stored

Removes your theme, red-letter and notification choices from this device. Your browser’s notification permission is separate — see below.

Notifications

Notifications are off unless you turn them on.

Optional

Get a morning reminder for today’s study

The automated sender runs once each morning and includes the study title and a link to read it.

  • Off unless you turn it on.
  • Turning it on asks your browser for permission, and you can refuse.
  • You can turn it off again here, or in your browser, at any time.

Permission belongs to your browser, not to this site — so if you ever block notifications there, this page cannot ask again.

Mistakes

If something here is wrong—a misquoted verse, a wrong date, or a factual claim that does not hold up—it should be fixed rather than defended. Online reports are not yet available; the Corrections page explains the policy.
